Relaxation

Finding time to take a break can be extremely difficult. Incorporating it into daily life can help you refocus and reduce stress. Here are techniques to relax your mind and body.

a) Relax your body

What do I need? Choose a place to sit and relax where no one will disturb you.

What do I do?

  1. Lie down or sit with your back straight and your feet on the floor. Close your eyes or focus on a spot in the distance.
  2. Clench your toes for a few seconds and release. Notice the difference.
  3. Tense your muscles as you breathe in and relax as you breathe out.
  4. Move up your body to thighs, stomach, shoulders, and hands, clenching and relaxing each muscle.
  5. Take a moment to relax, then slowly move and stand up when ready.

b) Try gentle physical activity

  1. Take a walk at your own pace, even a few minutes can help.
  2. Join a class such as yoga, pilates, or gentle stretching.
  3. Try seated exercises if busy or limited in mobility.

c) Focus on your breathing

What do I need? Just yourself

What do I do?

  1. Breathe in through your nose and out through your mouth. Keep shoulders down and relaxed.
  2. Place hand on your stomach; it rises as you breathe in and falls as you breathe out.
  3. Count while breathing: '1,2,3,4' in and '1,2,3,4' out. Adjust to what feels comfortable.

d) Get creative

Painting, drawing, crafts, music, dancing, baking, sewing. Focus on enjoying yourself, not the result.

What do I need?

  • Table or desk
  • Paper, pencils, pens, crayons
  • Optional: tape to hold paper

What do I do? Sit comfortably, draw a circle, fill patterns, focus on sensations, not the result.

e) Spend time in nature

Take a walk in green spaces, notice surroundings, explore slowly, focus on senses. Variations: open window or care for a plant.

f) Focus on your senses

Notice 5 things you see, 4 things you touch, 3 things you hear, 2 things you smell, 1 thing you taste. Ground yourself in the present moment.

g) Imagine somewhere relaxing

Use your imagination to transport yourself to a calm, peaceful place. Close your eyes and visualize details to relax.

h) Listen to music

Listen to your favorite songs, dance, sing along, or just enjoy. Focus on instruments and rhythm to distract from stress.

i) Take a screen break

Step away from technology. Turn off phone for an hour or avoid social media/email. Use the time for relaxing activities.
